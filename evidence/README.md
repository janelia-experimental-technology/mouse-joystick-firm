# Verification Evidence

The record of how the `mouse-joystick-pcb` v1.0 boards and this firmware were
verified, from the first v1.0 power-up on 2026-10-05 to the installed rig's
first observed experiment on 2026-10-09, the desk rig's bring-up on
2026-10-12, its reflash on 2026-10-14, Rig 1's reflash and check on
2026-10-15 and the desk rig's torque calibration against a force gauge on
2026-10-16: one directory per bench or rig session, named by date, time and
subject. Each holds the console logs,
protocol transcripts, pyControl session outputs, trace analyses and small
logic-analyzer captures of that session, with `meta.txt` naming the firmware
commit and build, the board serial and the setup. The measurements these
files support are written up, with their numbers, in the board repository's
bring-up log (`mouse-joystick-pcb/docs/bringup-log.md`), which cites the
files here as `mouse-joystick-firm/evidence/<session>/<file>`.

The sessions were driven from the sibling repository `qp-arduino-example`,
whose `evidence/` directory keeps the raw material in full (about 835 MB of
`.sr` and `.vcd` logic-analyzer captures). This copy keeps every text file,
the captures up to 1 MB and the four first-board captures the bring-up log
cites by name; each session's `OMITTED.sha256` lists what was left behind
with its size and hash so the raw files can be identified and requested.
`bench-scripts/` holds the shell scripts that drove the sessions, verbatim.

## What Was Verified

- **Every bring-up stage on both v1.0 boards**: stage 1 self test (20 checks),
  stage 2 TMC5130A register reads with the switched rail power-cycled,
  A3 the angle DAC over I2C, A6 motion, encoder tracking and homing, A4 the
  brake current sink (linear to 49 mA) and A5 the torque bridge
  (`2026-10-05-1142-board4-regression/v1/`, `2026-10-06-0848-unitA-brake-torque/`,
  `2026-10-07-0908-unitB-bringup/`).
- **Gate E**: pyControl 2.1.1 running the bring-up task on the pyboard against a
  board over the RJ-45, through pyControl's own board API
  (`2026-10-06-1019-unitA-gate-e/` and the runs after it; unit B on
  Breakout port 1 in `2026-10-07-0908-unitB-bringup/pilot/gate_e_left.txt`).
- **The sampler's tick budget** attributed on the bus and on the pin, and the
  flash stage-2 change that brought it back to 1 kHz
  (`2026-10-06-1104-unitA-tmc-spi-timing/`, `2026-10-06-1132-unitA-tick-attribution/`,
  `2026-10-06-1140/1147/1355-unitA-gate-e-fastflash/`).
- **Positioning**: the park error measured and attributed to lost motion on
  reversal, then the encoder settle that bounds every `TARGET` to 0.18°
  (`2026-10-06-1407-unitA-park-error/`, `2026-10-07-0807-unitA-encoder-settle/`).
- **The rig's own task**: eight trials dry, the reward path with a hand on the
  lever, both analog outputs on an oscilloscope, then 400 trials unattended
  with no fault (`2026-10-07-0840-…`, `-0855-…`, `-0950-…`, `-1040-unitA-soak/`).
- **The installed rig**: the laptop observer identifying which analyzer sits on
  which board and watching the desktop's mock experiment
  (`2026-10-09-0919-rig1-which-analyzer/`, `2026-10-09-0920-rig1-mock-experiment/`).
- **The desk rig**: two further v1.0 boards (units C and D) from the ROM
  bootloader through the stage 1 self test and the delivery build, `id` over
  both RJ-45 ports, one trial of the rig's task per board through the
  headless runner, and both pull signs by hand
  (`2026-10-12-1355-deskrig-bringup/`).
- **The torque chain against a known torque**: a Mark-10 M3-05 force gauge
  logged over USB beside the boards' 50 Hz stream while Peter loaded each
  desk-rig lever at five brake levels; with the gear out and the gauge on a
  string the sensor read 0.965 of the torque through the brake against 0.961
  expected from its certificate; friction in the lever axis 0.25–0.5 N·cm per
  station and the brake curve per station measured
  (`2026-10-16-0951-deskrig-torque-cal/`).
- **Link loss on hardware**: the task's reply timeouts exercised by unplugging
  the RJ-45 before a session (three resends, session ended at 2.0 s) and
  during a watch (ended at the deadline, board latched `host_timeout` with
  its rail off, next session recovered)
  (`2026-10-12-1355-deskrig-bringup/`).
